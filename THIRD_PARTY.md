## grill-me

The upstream `grill-me` (same source and commit) is a one-line entry point that calls `grilling`.
This repository replaces it with its own `skills/grill-me`, which keeps that role and adds the Goal Loop mode.
It is covered by this repository's license, not by the upstream one.
